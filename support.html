SEAL Support

Get help with SEAL.

Get help with sequence editing, analysis tools, and lab notes in SEAL. For online analyses, see the services and data SEAL sends.

Email kingtelomere@gmail.com for questions, bug reports, and feature requests.

System requirements

  • An Apple silicon Mac. SEAL is not built for Intel Macs, Windows, or Linux.
  • The minimum macOS version stated on the SEAL App Store listing.
  • Free disk space for the app and for your workspaces. The App Store listing states the download size.
  • No account, licence key, or internet connection to open the app and work on sequences.

The Mac App Store edition runs sandboxed. It has no terminal, installs and launches no external alignment, genome, or scoring tools, and contacts no AI provider. Edition differences compares what each edition includes.

Report a problem

Email the report to kingtelomere@gmail.com. Start with your SEAL version, your macOS version, and whether you installed from the App Store, a disk image, or source.

  1. Describe what you were trying to do.
  2. List the steps that produced the problem, and quote the error message.
  3. Say what you expected and what happened instead.

Use a small made-up sequence when the report needs one. Remove private sequences, credentials, personal file paths, and unrelated information from screenshots and attachments.

Report a security issue

Email kingtelomere@gmail.com and put Security in the subject line. Describe the issue, the SEAL version and macOS version you saw it on, and the steps to reproduce it.

Send no passwords, API keys, or private workspace data. Give the report time to be fixed before you publish it.

Common questions

Where does SEAL keep my data?

In a SQLite database named seal.db, inside a .seal folder in the workspace you selected. If you selected no workspace, SEAL uses a database in its own data folder. Preferences and folder permissions live in the app's local profile, separate from the database.

How do I remove SEAL and everything it stored?

Quit SEAL and any other tool using the same workspace. Delete the app. Then delete the .seal folder in each workspace you selected, along with any recovery copies and exported backups you no longer want. Deleting the app alone leaves workspace folders you chose elsewhere on disk untouched.

Do I need an AI provider key?

No. Sequence editing, annotation, cloning, primer design, trace reading, and the Notebook all run locally with no key and no network. A key is needed only for the hosted model feature in the direct-download edition. The Mac App Store edition stores no provider key and contacts no AI provider.

Where is my provider key stored?

The Mac App Store edition stores no key. In the direct-download edition, SEAL encrypts the key with the macOS Keychain and writes only the encrypted form to the app's data folder. The key never enters your workspace database, a backup, or an export. Delete it in Settings to remove it, and revoke it at the provider as well.

Does SEAL collect analytics or send crash reports?

No. Neither edition contains analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting, and neither makes a network request unless you start an action that needs one. The privacy policy lists every destination and the action that reaches it.

Can I move a workspace to another Mac?

Yes. Copy the workspace folder, including its .seal folder, and open it in SEAL on the other Mac. A stored provider key and your folder permissions stay behind, because macOS ties them to the Mac and the account that created them.

How do I get updates?

The Mac App Store delivers updates for the Mac App Store edition through the App Store app. SEAL never checks for updates on its own.

Privacy questions
